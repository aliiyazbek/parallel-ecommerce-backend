## 2. Bottleneck identified — N+1 queries

`ProductSerializer` exposes `category_name` through `source="category.name"`. Listing N products **without** `select_related("category")` issues **one query for the products plus one extra query per product** to fetch its category — the classic **N+1 query problem**. It scales linearly with catalogue size and is the dominant cost of the listing endpoint.

## 3. Before vs After the fix

Both variants serialize the same **200 products**, averaged over **30** runs.

| Variant | DB queries | Mean (ms) | p50 (ms) |
|---------|-----------:|----------:|---------:|
| **BEFORE** — no `select_related` | 201 | 71.30 | 69.16 |
| **AFTER** — `select_related("category")` | 1 | 12.59 | 11.34 |

### Improvement

| Metric | Before | After | Gain |
|--------|-------:|------:|:-----|
| DB queries | 201 | 1 | **−200 (100% fewer)** |
| Mean latency | 71.30 ms | 12.59 ms | **5.66× faster (−82%)** |

## 4. The fix

```python
# products/views.py — ProductViewSet
queryset = Product.objects.filter(is_active=True).select_related("category")
```
